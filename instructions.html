Simple Steps to Join the Fun

Getting set up is easy. Follow these to start quizzing and minting badges.

Step 1: Get MetaMask

Head to metamask.io and install the wallet. Make a new one and keep your secret phrase safe—it's like your key.

Step 2: Add Base Sepolia Network

In MetaMask, go to networks and add this one:

  • Name: Base Sepolia
  • RPC: https://sepolia.base.org
  • Chain ID: 84532
  • Symbol: ETH
  • Explorer: https://sepolia.basescan.org

Step 3: Grab Some Test ETH

Go to the Coinbase faucet at portal.cdp.coinbase.com/products/faucet. Connect your wallet and claim free test ETH. Give it a minute to show up.

Step 4: Jump into a Quiz

On the Quiz page, connect your wallet, pick a topic, answer the questions. Hit 80% or better, and mint your badge—it's that straightforward!
