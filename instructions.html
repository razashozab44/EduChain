Getting Started with STEM Chain

Follow these simple steps to connect your wallet, take quizzes, and earn your first blockchain-verified badge!

📱 Step 1: Set Up Your Wallet

Download MetaMask from metamask.io. Create a new wallet and securely save your secret recovery phrase—this is your key to everything.

🔗 Step 2: Add Base Sepolia Network

In MetaMask, add the Base Sepolia test network with these details:

  • Network Name: Base Sepolia
  • RPC URL: https://sepolia.base.org
  • Chain ID: 84532
  • Currency Symbol: ETH
  • Block Explorer: https://sepolia.basescan.org

💰 Step 3: Get Test ETH (Free!)

Visit the Coinbase Faucet, connect your MetaMask wallet, and claim free test ETH. This funds your quiz and badge minting!

🚀 Step 4: Take a Quiz & Earn Your Badge

Head to the Quiz page, connect your wallet, pick a STEM topic, and answer 10 questions. Score 80% or higher to unlock your blockchain badge!
