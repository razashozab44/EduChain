What We're All About

STEMChain came from seeing how fake certificates can mess up opportunities for students. We built this app at DPS Sahiwal to let kids prove their STEM skills with badges that are stored on the blockchain—meaning they're safe and real. It's easy: Take a quiz, score high, and get your badge. No more worries about fakes, and it's fun too!
