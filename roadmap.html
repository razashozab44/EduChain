Our Roadmap

We're building STEMChain step by step to make it even better. Here's what's coming up.

Q1 2026: Get Started Strong

Update the app with more quizzes and easy Base Chain integration. Make it work like a mini-app for quick play.

Q2 2026: Build the Community

Add chats, leaderboards, and ways to challenge friends. Share your badges easily on social media.

Q3 2026: Add Rewards

Introduce tokens you can earn from quizzes. Stake your badges to get extra perks and vote on new features.

Q4 2026+: Go Big

Work with more schools, add AI for custom quizzes, and expand to other blockchains. Host events like our exhibition every year.
