What is STEM Chain?

STEM Chain is a revolutionary learning platform that combines the power of STEM education with blockchain technology. Students can take quizzes on Physics, Chemistry, Biology, and Environmental Science. Upon successful completion, they earn digital badges minted on the Base Chain blockchain—credentials that are immutable, verifiable, and universally recognized.

Whether you're a high school student looking to boost your portfolio or simply passionate about learning, STEM Chain provides a secure, engaging platform to prove your expertise.
