Setup Guide: Get Ready to Learn & Mint!

Step 1: Create MetaMask Wallet

Download MetaMask from metamask.io. Create a new wallet (save seed phrase safely!).

Step 2: Add Base Sepolia Testnet

In MetaMask, click Networks → Add Network:

  • Name: Base Sepolia
  • RPC: https://sepolia.base.org
  • Chain ID: 84532
  • Symbol: ETH
  • Explorer: https://sepolia.basescan.org

Step 3: Get Free Test Tokens (Faucet)

Go to base.org/faucets. Connect MetaMask → Claim 0.5 ETH (free for testnet).

Tip: Wait 1-2 mins for confirmation.

Step 4: Start the Quiz

Connect wallet on this site, choose topic, answer 10 questions (80% to qualify), mint your NFT badge!
