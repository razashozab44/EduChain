Welcome to STEMChain

STEMChain is a simple way for students to learn science, technology, engineering, and math through fun quizzes. When you do well, you get a special digital badge on the Base Chain blockchain. These badges can't be faked, so they're great for showing off what you've learned to schools or jobs. It's all about making learning exciting and secure, especially for high school kids like us.
